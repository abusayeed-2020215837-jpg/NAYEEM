Material Synthesis and Characterization

Research Assistant | Materials Science Research Laboratory, University of Dhaka

My research focuses on the synthesis and characterization of functional semiconductor materials for energy and environmental applications. I prepare and optimize semiconductor samples using controlled experimental methods and investigate their structural, optical, and electronic properties. To accelerate materials discovery, I employ machine learning approaches for property prediction and data-driven analysis. The project combines experimental techniques with computational approaches, including density functional theory (DFT) simulations. The overall goal is to develop efficient functional materials for sustainable and next-generation technologies.

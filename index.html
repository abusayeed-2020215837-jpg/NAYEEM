Hi, I am Abu Sayeed Nayeem, an Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at the University of Dhaka. My research interests lie at the intersection of computational materials science, machine learning, and semiconductor technology. I focus on developing and discovering functional materials through machine learning-assisted approaches, density functional theory (DFT) simulations, and experimental characterization techniques.

My research journey revolves around designing advanced semiconductor materials for sustainable energy conversion, environmental remediation, and next-generation electronic applications. I am particularly interested in combining computational methods with experimental insights to accelerate materials discovery and understand the relationship between structure, properties, and performance.

I am currently working as a Research Assistant at the Materials Science Research Laboratory, University of Dhaka, where I contribute to research on semiconductor photocatalytic materials, computational modelling, and materials optimization. My work involves applying machine learning techniques for materials screening, performing DFT-based simulations, and evaluating functional materials through experimental studies.

Beyond research, I am passionate about continuous learning and exploring emerging technologies that connect artificial intelligence, materials science, and electronic engineering. My long-term goal is to contribute to the development of innovative materials and technologies that address challenges in energy, environment, and advanced electronics.
